Software as a research output

  • Research projects produce more than scientific publications and datasets


  • Software plays a central role in:

    • Data collection and acquisition
    • Data cleaning and transformation
    • Data analysis and visualisation
    • Modelling and simulation

  However, software is often less documented, shared and recognised than data or publications

What is a Software Paper?

  • A scientific peer-reviewed publication whose primary focus is a software application or tool


  • It describes:

    • the scientific context and the problem addressed
    • the purpose and main functionalities of the software
    • its architecture and technical design
    • the methods and algorithms implemented
    • installation requirements and dependencies

Why publish a Software paper?






Visibility

Make research software discoverable
by the scientific community


Recognition

Give credit to software developers
and contributors


Traceability

Establish a link between software
and scientific publications



Reproducibility

Document methods, workflows and
execution conditions


Reusability

Enable other teams to understand,
test and adapt the software

Software paper, Data paper and co






Research article

Why was the research conducted,
and what were the findings?

What data were produced, and how
are they structured and documented?


Methods paper

What method was developed, why was it designed, and how can it be applied?


Software paper

What software was developed, how
does it work, and how can it be reused?
